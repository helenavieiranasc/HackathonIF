Mode: Persuade. Single page, pt-BR. Audience: IFSP Jacareí students, many new to hackathons, mostly on phones. Action: register a team (external URL, placeholder) and find the challenge, schedule and rules fast. Proof on hand: none; every unconfirmed fact is a visible placeholder from content.js. ## Direction contract

THESIS: The hackathon is labelled like lab equipment. Every confirmed fact is a strip of embossed label-maker tape punched by the organizers; every unconfirmed fact is yellow tape still waiting for its letters. Refuses the centered dark hero over a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Light cool-gray bench laminate as ground, a dark steel cabinet for the challenge, white paper sheets for regulations. Tape colors carry roles: black for structure and navigation, IFSP green for actions, IFSP red for the Semana de Tecnologia plate and for deadlines, yellow for pending facts, blue for outbound links. Raised off-white letters in a fixed-pitch label-wheel face (Sometype Mono 700); running text in Switzer. Depth only from the tape itself: letters raised by an SVG light model, a short soft cast shadow, and a faint vinyl sheen with grain on each strip; no decorative gradients or glow.

STORY: A student sees HACKATHON punched onto tape, learns in one line it is the Semana de Tecnologia hackathon at IFSP Jacareí, understands what a hackathon is (HACK + MARATONA), sees the challenge drawer, follows the numbered route from registration to results, checks dates and rules, and ends by punching their own team name before registering.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White equipment plate top-left with the official IFSP logo; navigation as a row of small black tapes, register as green tape at right (MENU tape on phones). HACKATHON on one long black tape across about 70% of the width, rotated -2deg; red tape "SEMANA DE TECNOLOGIA" overlapping below at +1.5deg; one paragraph naming campus and theme placeholder; green "INSCREVER EQUIPE" tape as the primary action with "VER O DESAFIO" beside it; yellow deadline tape at right.
